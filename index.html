PACK THE BASICS

A simple emergency kit

Keep essentials in a place that is easy to reach. Adjust supplies to your household.

◉

Water

Keep drinking water for each person and refresh it regularly.

▤

Food

Choose familiar, non-perishable food and a manual opener if needed.

＋

First aid

Include basic first aid supplies and items your household uses.

☼

Torch

Keep a working torch and spare batteries together.

▰

Power bank

Charge it from time to time and keep its cable with it.

▧

Documents

Protect copies of important documents in a waterproof pouch.

♡

Medicines

Keep essential personal medicines and relevant instructions.

◌

Whistle

A whistle can help you signal for help if you are trapped.

KEEP THIS INFORMATION ACCESSIBLE

Emergency contacts

In India, 112 is the pan-India emergency response number. Call if you need urgent police, fire, or medical assistance.

For local services and other helplines, check the official website of your state or district. Do not rely on this learning project for live emergency updates.

Right now

  • Move away from immediate danger if you can do so safely.
  • Follow instructions from local authorities.
  • Avoid damaged buildings, downed wires, and moving floodwater.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are, if possible.

This is general guidance, not a substitute for emergency services.
